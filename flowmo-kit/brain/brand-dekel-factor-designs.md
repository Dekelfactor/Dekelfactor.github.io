# Brand: Dekel Factor Designs

**Positioning:** "I make complex products feel simple." Product designer (UX/UI) for apps, websites and product videos. Designs from research, not guesses.
**Audiences:** founders & startups, small businesses, companies with complex products, brands that need video; recruiters on LinkedIn.
**Voice:** short, human, first person. Story first, pitch last. No claims of metrics or results that were not measured.

## Colour
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#050507` (near-black) |
| "Without" world | cold blue-grey grade, desaturated |
| "With" world | warm red / amber, stage haze |
| Caption accent (Reels) | `#FF4919` red-orange |
| Product accent | taken from the product's own logo (Open Stage Jam `#FB0D1F`) |
| Text | `#FFFFFF`, secondary `#BEAAA0` |

## Type
- **Kinetic captions:** heavy all-caps sans (Montserrat ExtraBold / Inter Display Black), white, key word on a solid accent-red highlight bar, 2 lines max, lower third (y ≈ 1430–1560 of 1920), pop + slight rise.
- **Context tag:** small pill at top centre — "WITHOUT THE APP" (grey) / "with [product logo]" (dark pill, red rim).
- **App label pills:** white pill, dark text, Inter Display ExtraBold 30 px, under each floating phone.
- **LinkedIn end card:** use the product's own type system (Open Stage Jam: Bebas Neue headline, Space Grotesk body, Space Mono labels).

## Motion & edit
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, ~40 s. Cuts on the music; whip / flash / glitch transitions; punch-ins.
- One **black beat** of near-silence right before the end card; the end card is the loudest hit.
- Loudness −14 to −16 LUFS, peak ≤ −1 dBFS. Keep all text inside x 60–940, y 250–1500 (platform UI).

## Real product screens
- Only real screens, never invented UI. One screen per beat, as a **floating 3D phone**: ~18° perspective turn, fine light rim, faint glass sheen, soft shadow; **no glow, no grey edge slab**. Swing in with a spring, slow sway, label pill below.

## End cards (4 s)
- **Reels/TikTok:** Paper-me head → 3D glossy lettering "Comment" (cream) + keyword (red lacquer) → "and I'll send you the …" → small line "case study · portfolio · Dekel Factor Designs" → red pill **Link in bio**.
- **LinkedIn:** stage-light beam onto the product logo → white Bebas headline with one red accent word → outline pill "Case study in the first comment" → divider → byline (Paper-me head + "Dekel Factor Designs" + "OPEN TO PRODUCT DESIGN ROLES").
