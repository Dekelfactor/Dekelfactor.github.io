# Dekel Factor Designs — 20s cut-paper short

**Idea:** Dekel Factor Designs takes a small idea and makes it a brand people see.
**Device:** the hero (the user's own Paper-me head sticker) rides every step: sketch → cut → build → launch, and ends as the studio's mark.
**Format:** F2 morph chain (proven). 3 morphs (6.0, 10.5, 18.0, each 0.8s) and one hard cut on the payoff (15.5).
**Style:** cut paper. 9:16 1080×1920, 24fps, 120 BPM, music only.

| # | time | beat | bridge out | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–1.5 | night desk, the hero asleep on a blank sheet | lamp clicks | faint drone |
| 2 | 1.5–6 | sketch: pencil circles the hero, notes pop, it wakes | ring → badge | pad, motif, pencil |
| 3 | 6–10.5 | cut: scissors cut the badge from pink paper; it lifts | badge → browser | 8th plucks, snips |
| 4 | 10.5–14.5 | build: the real Open Stage Jam app on two paper phones; click on "View Event" | — | fullest 16ths |
| 5 | 14.5–15.5 | hold: push in on the hero | hard cut | silence |
| 6 | 15.5–18 | launch: shopfront sign with the hero, Open Stage Jam poster, full-body Paper-me cheering, confetti | sign → sheet | stab + sub (loudest), flat chord |
| 7 | 18–20 | morning desk: the mark on the sheet, "Dekel Factor Designs", Paper-me waving | loops | pad, motif |

**Claims on screen:** the studio's name; "like Open Stage Jam, a live jam app" — the user's own app (Dekelfactor/openstagejam, captured from its index.html at 390×844). No dates or "first" claims.
