What the app stores, and where

  • Your project (layers, keyframes, settings) is saved in your browser's local storage on this device, so it survives a reload.
  • Media you import (images, video, audio, 3D models) is kept in your browser's IndexedDB on this device.
  • Two small preferences: preview quality, and whether you have seen the orbit hint.

None of this is sent to us. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels, and no cookies.

Network requests

The app loads its own files (code, fonts, icons) from this website. Fonts are hosted here, not on a third-party font service. If a project refers to media at an https:// address, your browser fetches it from that address, and that site can see your IP address.

Our hosting provider, [ACTION REQUIRED: confirm host, e.g. Netlify], processes your IP address and request details to serve the site and may keep server logs. See their privacy policy for how long.

Clipboard and Claude

The app reads or writes your clipboard only when you tap a copy or paste button. "Copy project for Claude" puts your project text on the clipboard; you decide whether to paste it into a conversation with Claude, which is covered by Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. The app itself never contacts Anthropic or any AI service.

Rendering on your computer

MP4 export runs on your own computer with the included render script. Exported project files contain your media, so treat them like the originals.

Deleting your data

Open the project menu and choose Delete all data on this device. This erases the saved project, imported media and preferences from this browser. Clearing site data in your browser settings does the same.
