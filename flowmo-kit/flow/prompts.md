In the foreground [hero] sits alone at the bar counter, an acoustic guitar case leaning beside him, waiting and looking toward the stage. Shallow depth of field.
2. **Chaos** (without): [hero] stares anxiously at his phone in a dark bar. Around him float many translucent glass message bubbles and social-post cards with big question marks, overlapping chaotically in the air. The phone screen glows but its content is not legible. Bokeh bar lights behind.
3. **Clock** (without): Late at night in a bar: [hero] slumps at the bar counter, chin on his hand, bored and tired, empty glasses in front of him, guitar case beside him. A large analog wall clock above the bar shows half past eleven. Other performers blurred on a stage in the background.
4. **Rain** (without): A rainy night street outside a small music bar with a warm glowing doorway behind. [hero] walks away alone toward the camera, carrying his guitar case, head down, disappointed. Wet reflective pavement, light rain. No readable signs.
5. **Scan** (with): A warm, lively live-music bar at night. On a small wooden table stands a little table tent card with a QR code. [hero] holds his phone up to scan it, smiling, his guitar case beside him, friends blurred around. Phone screen glows, not legible.
6. **Relaxed** (with): Same warm red-lit music bar. [hero] sits relaxed at a table with a drink, laughing with two other musicians, his phone lying face up on the table glowing softly. A singer performs on the stage behind them.
7. **Buzz** (with): Close-up of a smartphone on a wooden bar table lighting up with a bright notification glow (screen not legible), small vibration ripples on a drink beside it. In the background, slightly blurred, [hero] stands up excited, grabbing his acoustic guitar.
8. **Stage** (with, hero shot; reference = cheering body): [hero] on a small stage singing into a vintage microphone with an acoustic guitar, eyes closed, joyful. Deep red stage lights, haze and light beams; a glossy red glass ring-shaped light sculpture floating above the stage. In the foreground, a cheering crowd in silhouette with raised hands and phones.

**Turn** (warm red): [hero] at a jam night with a notebook, talking with musicians; behind him a wall of sticky notes, a persona card and a user-flow sketch.
**Close** (warm, café at night): [hero] across a café table from a founder, their app glowing on a laptop between them, ending in a handshake.
