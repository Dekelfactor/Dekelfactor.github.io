# Product: Open Stage Jam (template for every case-study product)

**One line:** A real-time queue app for open mic and jam nights: performers find the room, prepare their set once, and see exactly when they're on.
**Pain (without):** scattered posts, no reply, invisible place in line, waiting all night and never performing.
**Research (true, from the case study):** six years of jam nights; ~30 conversations; persona Alex ("I'm ready to perform. I just need to know what happens next.").
**Case study:** dekelfactordesigns.com/work/open-stage.html · **CTA keyword:** JAM

**Brand:** logo = the app's welcome-screen SVG (red circle + mic, "Open stage", script "Jam"), red `#FB0D1F` with a red glow. Type: Bebas Neue / Space Grotesk / Space Mono.
