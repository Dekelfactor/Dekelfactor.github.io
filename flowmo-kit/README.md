# Dekel Factor Designs — channel kit for Flowmo

Everything that made the Open Stage Jam client ad, packaged so every new video comes out in the same style.
Built to load into **Flowmo** as three Brain records + one reusable Flowgen flow.

| File | Goes into Flowmo as |
|---|---|
| `brain/brand-dekel-factor-designs.md` | Brain → **Brand** record (colours, type, voice, caption + end-card rules) |
| `brain/character-paper-me.md` | Brain → **Character** record (the Paper-me host, with its reference images) |
| `brain/product-open-stage-jam.md` | Brain → **Product** record (one per case study; copy it for the next product) |
| `flow/channel-video-flow.md` | Flowgen canvas → **Save as flow** "Client story reel" (the node tree, node by node) |
| `flow/prompts.md` | The exact prompts, as Text nodes wired into the generators |
| `assets/` (zip, not in git) | Brain references + Assets tab: Paper-me cutouts, story stills, app screens, logo, end-card lettering |

## The formula every video follows (40 s, 9:16, music only, captions carry the story)

1. **Cold open** (0–3 s): the pain as a question, inside the story — "Waited all night to sing… and never got on?"
2. **Without** (3–11 s), cold blue: 4 beats of the pain, one 2-word caption each.
3. **Same night.** (11–12 s): a rewind flicker.
4. **With** (12–19.5 s), warm red: 4 beats of the product working; one real app screen per beat as a floating 3D phone + label pill.
5. **The turn** (19.5–25 s): "That's how a confusing product feels to your users." → "I start where your users are."
6. **Close** (25–36 s): client meeting → "I make complex products feel simple."
7. **Black beat** (~0.5 s), then **end card** (36.75–40.75 s): Reels = 3D "Comment ___"; LinkedIn = product logo + one headline + byline.

Reusing it: duplicate the flow, swap the **Product** record and the 8 story beats; keep the Brand and Character records.
